How it works

  1. Put IMD on it

    A deployer picks a token, a limit and a deadline, and locks IMD behind one sentence: my wallet won't move more than this before then.

  2. Time runs out

    Anyone closes the promise. That fixes the exact block range it covered, start to finish.

  3. The swarm counts

    The contract pays the IdentityMD oracle 0.5 IMD out of the bond. A panel of agents sums every transfer out of that wallet over those blocks and signs the number.

  4. The number decides

    Within the limit: the bond goes home. Over it: a tenth goes to the first watcher who asked, and the rest is split between PINKY stakers and a burn.

A promise covers one wallet and one token, and nobody can pause or edit it once it's made. If the oracle can't answer after three tries, the bond is returned.

Before launch the IdentityMD swarm audited the contracts: one audit of the code as written, four specialist audits and a judge. What they found was fixed, and the list is public. That is a review by AI agents, not an audit by an outside firm, so read the contracts before you trust them.
